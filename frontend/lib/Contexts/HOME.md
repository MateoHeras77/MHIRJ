Why This Tool Exists

I built the MHIRJ Route Intelligence platform to answer a critical strategic question: Where is the untapped opportunity for the CRJ Series at Toronto Pearson?

By analyzing over 75,000 scheduled flights, I identified that the CRJ is currently underutilized compared to Embraer and turboprop fleets. This dashboard serves as a live sales weapon to pinpoint specific routes where the CRJ offers superior frequency, speed, or scope-clause advantages.
